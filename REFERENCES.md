# Awesome DSA Academic & Engineering References

A curated index of seminal research papers, foundational textbooks, and production engineering blogs referenced across this repository.

---

## 1. Seminal Papers

### Trees & Hierarchies
* Bayer, R., & McCreight, E. (1970). *"Organization and Maintenance of Large Ordered Indices"*. Acta Informatica. (The foundational B-Tree paper).
* Guibas, L. J., & Sedgewick, R. (1978). *"A Dichromatic Framework for Balanced Trees"*. IEEE FOCS. (Introduction of Red-Black Trees).
* Sleator, D. D., & Tarjan, R. E. (1985). *"Self-Adjusting Binary Search Trees"*. Journal of the ACM. (Introduction of Splay Trees).
* Pugh, W. (1990). *"Skip Lists: A Probabilistic Alternative to Balanced Trees"*. Communications of the ACM.

### External Memory & Storage
* O'Neil, P., Cheng, E., Gawlick, D., & O'Neil, E. (1996). *"The Log-Structured Merge-Tree (LSM-Tree)"*. Acta Informatica.
* Frigo, M., Leiserson, C. E., Prokop, H., & Ramachandran, S. (1999). *"Cache-Oblivious Algorithms"*. IEEE FOCS.

### Probabilistic & Streaming
* Bloom, B. H. (1970). *"Space/Time Trade-offs in Hash Coding with Allowable Errors"*. Communications of the ACM.
* Flajolet, P., Fusy, É., Gandouet, O., & Meunier, F. (2007). *"HyperLogLog: the analysis of a near-optimal cardinality estimation algorithm"*. AOFA.
* Cormode, G., & Muthukrishnan, S. (2005). *"An improved data stream summary: the count-min sketch and its applications"*. Journal of Algorithms.
* Fan, B., Andersen, D. G., Kaminsky, M., & Mitzenmacher, M. (2014). *"Cuckoo Filter: Practically Better Than Bloom"*. ACM CoNEXT.
