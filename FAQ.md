# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What makes Awesome DSA different from other DSA repositories?
Most repositories are either:
1. Flat, unvetted lists of links without explanations.
2. Code dumps of LeetCode solutions without underlying intuition.

**Awesome DSA** is structured as an **Open-Source Encyclopedia & Engineering Handbook**. Every topic couples mathematical foundations and invariants with visual mental models, CPU cache/hardware realities, and production systems case studies (e.g. Linux CFS scheduler, PostgreSQL B-Trees, RocksDB LSM-Trees).

---

### 2. How should I navigate this repository?
* **For Beginners & Students**: Follow the [Beginner Learning Path](docs/00-learning-paths/beginner-path.md) starting from [Mathematical Foundations](docs/01-mathematical-foundations/) and [Linear Structures](docs/04-linear-data-structures/).
* **For Interview Preparation**: Jump directly to [Problem-Solving Patterns](docs/19-problem-solving-patterns/) and the [Interview Path](docs/00-learning-paths/interview-path.md).
* **For Systems Engineers**: Dive into [Machine Model & Performance](docs/03-machine-model-and-performance/) and [Systems Case Studies](docs/18-systems-case-studies/).
* **For Competitive Programmers**: Explore [Range Query Structures](docs/12-range-query-and-offline-structures/) and [Advanced Data Structures](docs/14-advanced-data-structures/).

---

### 3. Which programming languages are supported?
Our conceptual documentation emphasizes **Python** (for high-level readability and algorithm clarity) and **Modern C++** (for memory layout, pointer mechanics, and CPU cache performance). Our `implementations/` directory also welcomes idiomatic code in **Rust**, **Go**, and **Java**.

---

### 4. How can I contribute?
Check out our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md), pick an uncompleted topic in the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md), follow our [Style Guide](STYLE-GUIDE.md), and submit a Pull Request!
